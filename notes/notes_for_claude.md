# Board notes

The inner ward can be described as a 20 x 18 grid (many of which are not valid squares). I'm going to list the positions of all valid squares. If I don't mention some position coordinates you can assume that's an invalid square (make them green for grass).

Here I define it such that (1, 1) is the corner closest to the bell tower, (20, 1) is closest to the salt tower, etc (neither of those squares are valid btw). The valid squares are as follows (all ranges are inclusive):

- The Queen's house  is one space and spans (2, 1) to (6, 2) and connects to the yellow square behind it and to the white square at (5, 3)
- (2, 3) to (8, 3) are all white squares and (7, 3) specifically is circled (circled means no raven card I'm sure you recall)
- (7, 4) to (8, 9) are white squares
- (2, 4) to (2, 13) are all white squares, with circles on (2, 5) and (2, 12)
- (1, 8) to (8, 8) are white squares, a circle on (3, 8), (1, 8) connects to the yellow square immediately after the Beauchamp tower (before you get to the bell tower)
- The Scaffold post is one space (a Yeoman warder spot) and spans (7, 10) and (8, 10), it connects to the two white squares below it and those above it too. I.e. it blocks the path fully when there's a warder there.
- The Chapel post is one space (a Yeoman warder spot) and spans (1, 14) and (2, 14)
- (2, 15) to (2, 18) are white squares, (2, 17) is circled, (2, 18) connects to the Devereux tower
- The Chapel Royal of St Peter Ad Vincula is one space but covers (3, 16) to (7, 17) and connects to (2, 16) as well as the Salt Tower (via a secret passageway!)
- (3, 14) to (11, 14) are white squares, (7, 14) and (11, 14) are circled
- (7, 11) to (8, 13) are white squares, (8, 11) and (7, 12) are circled
- (8, 15) to (8, 18) are white squares, (8, 18) connects to the yellow square two spaces behind the flint tower (between it on the Bowyer tower)
- (9, 18) to (20, 18) are white squares, (11, 18), (14, 18) and (16, 18) are circled. (20, 18) connects to the Martin Tower
- (20, 14) to (20, 17) are white squares, (20, 16) is circled
- (10, 13) is a bench (should be brown)
- The Waterloo Post is one space (a Yeoman warder spot) and spans (12, 13) to (12, 14)
- (13, 13) is a white square
- (13, 14) to (19, 14) are white squares, (14, 14) is circled
- (17, 3) to (17, 13) are white squares, (17, 4), (17, 7), and (17, 13) are circled
- (18, 12) is a white square
- The Museum (this gives you a tower card) is one space and spans (19, 11) to (20, 13) and connects to (18, 12) only
- (18, 10) to (20, 10) are white squares (19, 10) is circled, (20, 10) connects to the yellow square two spaces behind the constable tower (between it and the Broad arrow tower)
- (16, 9) is a bench and connects only to (17, 9)
- (18, 8) is a white square
- The Hospital is one space that spans (19, 7) to (20, 9) and connects only to (18, 8)
- (18, 4) is a white square
- The Royal Armouries is one space that spans (19, 3) to (20, 5) and connects only to (18, 4)
- The Lanthorn post is one space (yeoman warder) and spans (16, 2) to (17, 2)
- (16, 3) and (16, 4) are white squares
- (14, 4) is a white square
- (14, 2) to (15, 4) are all white squares
- (13, 1) to (13, 2) are white squares

Another thing I should have mentioned, once you enter the White Tower, you must keep going in the same direction, you cannot go backwards and cannot be moved by Raven cards, or by other players. White Tower squares are (ironically) pink. The white tower squares proceed in this order:
- (12, 2), (11, 2), (10, 2), (10, 3), (11, 3), (12, 3), (13, 3), (13, 4), (12, 4), (11, 4), (11, 5)
- (11, 6) - This is the Sword (displayed one square the right, at (12, 6), same with the rest of the jewels, always one square to the right)
- (11, 7) - This is the Sceptre
- (11, 8) - This is the Orb
- (11, 9) - This is the Crown
- (11, 10) - This is the other crown
- (11, 11), (12, 11)
- (13, 11) - This square sends you to the Rack! It is also connected to (13, 13) via a rope (players can make this move if they have the rope, the rope is not discarded)
- (14, 11), (14, 10), (14, 9), (14, 8)
- The Chapel of St John is a single space but spans (13, 5) to (15, 7). It connects to (14, 8) and (14, 4). It also connects to (16, 4) via a ladder (similar to the rope above).

Lastly, there's some of the "inner ward", which goes outside. 
- (7, 2) is a white square
- (7, -3) to (7, 1) is a single space labelled "slide" (since it goes under the wall walk in 3D)
- (8, -3) is a white square
- (9, -3) to (11, -3) is a single space (another slide)
- (12, -3) is a space that gives you an extra turn
- (13, -3) to (18, -3) are white squares, and (13, -3), (14, -3), (16, -3), and (17, -3) are all circled
- (18, -3) connects to the Cradle Tower (the exit!). Players landing here with a coin and at least one jewel bank their jewel, return their coin, redraw the same starting number of cards as the start of the game (after shuffling the tower cards with their old cards included). If it's the short version that ends the game.
- (18, -2) to (18, 1) is a single square (another slide)
- And FINALLY (18, 2) is a circled white square that connects to the Lanthorn post
